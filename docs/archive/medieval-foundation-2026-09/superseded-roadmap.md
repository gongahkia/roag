# Superseded foundation roadmap

The former root plan treated progressively richer generation, simulation, provenance, renderer parity, management views, frontier expansion, and era work as ordered delivery phases. Those completed slices remain retained implementation history, but their order no longer defines the product.

The replacement decision is deliberate: do not add new frontier regions, full-person or distant-person fidelity, catch-up, delegation/autonomy/social-memory generality, NG+/NG++ eras, detailed rendering, management-sidebar breadth, content frameworks, schema/provenance layers, or large-world optimization until one courier expedition has been personally played and evaluated.

The new operating gate is: **The project owner completes and evaluates three full expeditions before scope expands.**
