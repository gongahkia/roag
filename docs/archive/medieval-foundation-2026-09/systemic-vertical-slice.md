# Historical slice rationale: Hearthford Mill Lease

The fixed Jomon deck had a real physical tally, cargo rack, typed causal history, and local market bands, but no consequential browser-facing loop connecting them. The Mill Lease slice joined those existing facts without adding a shop, route system, generic NPC contract, currency, or settlement simulation.

The selected incident is seed-derived (`sluice-jam` or `silted-intake`) and marks one reachable quay worksite. After accepting the one ironwork burden at Hearthford Mill Quay and delivering it to Jomon’s hold, the courier makes one bounded choice:

- Fit ironwork consumes the sound delivered lot, advances 20 minutes, relieves the named Mill Lease, and lowers ironwork pressure.
- Take lease credit retains the lot, advances 35 minutes, and records an owed Mill Lease obligation with continued pressure.

The state is replayed through the existing time-bearing causal command and persists in the existing world envelope. It owns no geometry, price, route, hidden person, timer, free prose, or second persistence authority. This narrow slice is complete; its evidence is in [`v19-mill-lease-verification.md`](v19-mill-lease-verification.md).
