# Retained medieval foundation

The September foundation established the parts Jomon keeps while the first expedition is built: deterministic creation, action-driven time, an active courier selected from a persistent household, local IndexedDB persistence, causal history, a physical keyboard-first Jomon deck, source-backed contextual prompts, cargo, bounded market facts, courier continuity, and content-safety boundaries.

It also accumulated systems that are intentionally retained but frozen: frontier generation, detailed/distant-person simulation, fidelity and catch-up, delegation, autonomy, social memory, eras, detailed rendering, broad management views, and supporting provenance/performance machinery. Their existence is not evidence that the game already has a satisfying playable loop.

The foundation remains valuable only insofar as it supports a concrete courier expedition. New work should prefer the smallest bounded state needed by that loop and must not reopen this historical plan by default.
