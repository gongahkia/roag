# Native migration tuning

Outdoor perception (2026-11-03): daylight is 06:00 inclusive to 20:00 exclusive on the existing 300-tick hour/7,200-tick day. Base surface radius is 11 by day and six at night; rain reduces it by one, storm/snow by three, floor four. Underground remains 11. These apply only when the attached native world clock drives survival; standalone combat clocks preserve static geometry. Both party and enemy private sight/target acquisition/local engagement use `VisionFrom` → `SightRadiusAt` and the same saved world tick/regional weather. No independent wall clock or visibility RNG is added. `CalendarDescription` derives day/hour/minute from that same tick; twelve ordinary 100-cost/100-speed waits advance one 1,200-unit world tick across dawn, verified with exact continuing save. Natural rendered outdoor transitions, lighting/presentation and ecological activity remain required.


Native trade timing (2026-11-02): one confirmed exchange costs 100 recovery at the acting member's current speed, for one finite batch of 1–999 units; free UI quantities are 1/5/10/50. Existing surface-town proximity (28 tiles), stock-based buy prices and `max(1, price / 2)` sale prices remain unchanged. Trade uses shared inventory and real finite town stocks, including existing exported/imported resource counters. Only the trader's local engagement blocks an exchange. Currency received by selling uses their currency-collected boon event; buying emits currency-spent. Food purchases do not trigger eating or health-supply behavior. Quotes reject integer capacity overflow before any side effect. Physical market journeys remain required after world cutover.


## Retained hostile ability conversion (2026-11-02)

The separate retained controller reads imported character/boss/variant behavior declarations; the original 50-enemy Roag catalog, placement pools and controllers remain unchanged. Imported stats use fixed world roles: ceiling(authored variant HP / 3), ceiling(power / 2), ceiling(armor / 2), speed `10 + 15 × authored speed`. Variant health/power/armor/speed modifiers apply before conversion. Registration preserves actual health ratio, persistent identity, equipment and source atlas sprite. Neither party level nor party size scales stats. Reward/site/population wiring remains separate pending integration work; the initial controller's 4/24 XP values are provisional and do not establish preserved imported rewards.

Each creature has an independent saved Park–Miller decision stream. Preview/inspection clone it. Only a committed cast consumes the resulting RNG and advances the cast-attempt counter; moving/searching/waiting does neither. Authored `every`/`fromRound` rules use this counter in the continuous timeline. An interrupted committed attempt still counts, but `noRepeat` compares the last **released** ability, matching original interruption behavior. Authored weights, conditions and current-HP thresholds remain intact. Boss normal/enraged selection is derived from current HP at each decision and reverses after healing above its authored threshold. Variant rules append to either set.

Weakest/strongest mean absolute current HP, with actor identity breaking ties, as in the source. Stressed means highest stress, then identity. Random selects uniformly from eligible privately seen candidates. Marked-target, self-wounded and ally-wounded conditions filter actual candidates; recovery cannot use another member's sight. Inspection describes the current source rules, weights, targeting, cadence, conditions and phase threshold. Imported legacy encounters targeted conscious units, so these candidates remain conscious. Shared awareness/search/navigation retain only an actual party observation, never a support target or a hidden current position.

All casts lock physical cells and use native windup/impact/recovery, not legacy battle turns. Ordinary windup is 100 cost; each authored preparation turn adds 100. Recovery is `100 + former mana × 10`, speed-scaled, with no mana consumption. Adjacent strike/rend/sting/nip/interrupt range is one tile; ranged single-target skills use six, all-hostile casts four, support three. Walls, diagonal corners, mist, floors and ordinary projectile bodies block as appropriate. Shots store their travel path; mirrored travel follows its committed turn. Moving from a marked cell dodges; later obstruction prevents impact. Support locks recipient cells rather than following the recipient.

Direct power is `max(1, actor power + ceiling((authored ability power − 6) / 3))`; healing is ceiling(authored power / 3). Native hit confirmation gates mark/bleed/burn/stun/dread. Mark retains authored power/duration. Bleed is three power per target own readiness for authored duration, reduced by prepared eggs; this remains distinct from downed bleed-out. Fire is three power for authored duration, using the **same scheduled actor burn** as boon/player fire: refresh retains already committed due/interval, extends remaining ticks, takes maximum power and replaces provenance. Broth resistance affects the real ticks. Dread uses `max(8, authored power)` stress minus tea/resolve. Stun uses up to two authored turns converted to seconds and really cancels locked work/delays readiness. Drain heals half actual loss, minimum one even after a fully blocked confirmed hit, preserving executed source behavior. Guard fixes its direction when committing the windup, rather than following moving prey at impact; it is a deliberate paid facing commitment; rally removes eight stress and gives a finite two-point ward until own readiness instead of automatic omnidirectional Guard. Purify removes ongoing bleed/burn/mark, clears the stun flag, calms ten and heals; already committed recovery remains paid.

These are controller/backend choices. Natural populations, retained rewards, living-world acquisition and rendered encounters remain acceptance work.
