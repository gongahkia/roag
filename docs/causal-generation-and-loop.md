# Causal generation and playable loop

Jomon derives a few consequential facts from named SHA-256 seed stages:

```text
river condition and terrain
→ Hearthford's work
→ commodity shortage
→ named contact's material interest
→ recovery or sluice-repair objective
→ route obstruction, flood crossing, and resource placement
→ market, relationship, route, injury, death, and succession consequences
```

The regional layout is authored and connected. The seed selects the condition,
needed commodity, useful side resource, contact, obstruction profile, objective
resource location, and rare finite ward. Each selected fact is shown to the
player and changes an available choice in the current expedition.

The playable loop is: choose one of six adult household members aboard Jomon;
choose one of three two-item loadouts and one of three support preparations;
cross the gangplank; meet the contact; accept, refuse, or materially alter the
request; take the direct towpath or operate the southern flood control; fight,
negotiate, or achieve positional evasion; recover, deliver, retain, or lose
physical goods; and walk back through the gangplank. The changed market,
contact memory, route, cargo, injury, death, succession, and bounded household
history are stored in the single JSON save and remain visible on a later trip.
