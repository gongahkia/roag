# Causal generation and playable loop

Jomon derives a few consequential facts from named SHA-256 seed stages:

```text
river condition and terrain
→ Hearthford's work
→ commodity shortage and named contact's material interest
→ recovery or mill-control objective
→ settlement hub, branching Reedwood, and linear mill route
→ hazards, resources, discoveries, threats, and merchant stock
→ market, relationship, room, equipment, injury, death, and succession consequences
```

Three direct topology functions construct a bounded twelve-room network. The
Hearthford hub has bounded spokes and an optional cross-link; Reedwood has two
branches, a required reconnection, and a possible second link; the millworks
has a four-room critical path and one side room attached to one of three
places. Authored terrain then gives every room a stable name, reciprocal
physical exits, and a gameplay purpose. The seed selects the regional
condition, commodity need, contact, cross-links, side-room name and attachment,
terrain details, discoveries, and rare finite relic or elite possibility.
Every selected fact is visible and changes a current route, build, encounter,
resource, or later exchange.

The playable loop is: use Jomon's tavern to choose one of six adult household
members, a weapon, secondary item, crew preparation, and optional finite relic;
cross the gangplank; meet the contact; accept, refuse, or materially alter the
request; choose and backtrack through settlement, wilderness, and industrial
rooms; fight, negotiate, evade through positioning, or control the material
environment; discover practical gear, knowledge, trade goods, consumables, or
rare embodied relics; recover, deliver, retain, consume, or lose physical
goods; report back; and walk through the gangplank. Changed rooms, market,
contact memory, equipment, cargo, injury, death, succession, and bounded
household history share one JSON save. A deterministic merchant may visit after
a return, deriving three offers from the regional need and previous outcome.
