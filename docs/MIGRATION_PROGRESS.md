## Handwritten implementation completed so far

`src/Roag.Core/Tactics/` contains:

- `ClassCatalog`: all 15 authored class definitions, original stats/costs/action IDs. Used by production world class enumeration/name lookup.
- `AttackGeometry`: pure targeting in world coordinates and floors, strict projectile corners, class attack footprints, companion targeting. Used by the replacement engine; the client still uses the old adapter pending remaining handlers.
- `ActionTiming`: original integer speed/recovery formula, with widened arithmetic.
- `ActionTimeline`: deterministic due/impact/readiness ordering, player priority, stable sequence IDs, cancellation, defensive snapshots and validated restore. The replacement engine now drives world ticks from this clock through `WorldTacticalSimulation`.
- `RoagRandom`: original Park–Miller stream, unbiased bounded draws and UTF-8 stream derivation.
- `CharacterBuild`: immutable per-member class/XP/level/permanent stats/random state, original six-stat growth, validated snapshots and explicit health-growth deltas. Attached to `Actor.Tactics` in the replacement engine; current HP remains solely on `Actor`. Contribution accounting and acquisition/reward routing are unfinished.
- `TacticalCombat`, `TacticalState`, `TacticalValidation`: replacement command/preview execution, queued locked attacks, friendly fire, post-defense directional Guard, rescue, bleed-out and validated persistence. Every participant is the existing world actor, using actual chunk terrain. Current supported commands include cardinal movement, class dash/bump behavior, wait, Guard, rescue, basic attacks, all class-specific specials and explicit authored windups. The complete boon/terrain/interaction pipelines, enemy decision-making and reward handlers still need ports before client cutover; basic handlers alone do not establish full class parity.
- `ClassResources`, `TacticalClassActions`, `TacticalTerrain`: independently owned Duelist parry/paid riposte, Shade decoy/swap, Trapper snare, Hookrunner pull, Stormcaller charge/delayed discharge/ward, commanded spirit, finite autonomous sentry, Geomancer bulwark and Wayfinder beacon. Construct effects run on the shared timeline offscreen; sentries and bulwarks persist after their owner's death while another party member survives. Bulwarks preserve terrain connectivity, block movement/projectiles/sight without rewriting the ground, and expire at their original timestamp. Wayfinder returns use explored routes of at most six tiles within one generated room; landing emits a single forced entry rather than traversal through intervening cells. Save validation rejects orphaned effects and forged beacon room identities.
- `Boons/BoonCatalog`, `BoonDefinition`, `BoonPredicate`, `BoonDefinitionValidation`: immutable typed definitions and subscription indexes for all 103 original boons, with original ordered pools and schema capabilities. Native declarations live in `assets/tactics/boons.json`; old sprite indices are excluded. This is content data parsed by handwritten C#, not generated rule code. Metadata/authoring validation covers all 24 triggers, 24 effect kinds, 60 predicates, eight patterns, seven weapon forms and 11 modifier kinds. Effect execution and native authoring CLI integration remain unfinished.
- `Boons/BoonInventory`, `ActorBoons`, `BoonChance`: member-owned immutable rarity stacks with class/conflict validation, permanent acquisition API, original chance-overflow arithmetic/RNG consumption, timed memory, one-hit ward consumption, and dice/vow primitives. `Actor.Tactics.Boons` is persisted and validated. Speed bonuses now affect native previews and committed recovery; wards absorb damage after class protection/directional Guard. Wards and speed expire inclusively at their original timestamp and do not rewrite already committed recovery. Source-event activation and dice/vow attack commitment are connected to native commands. Boon selection/receipts and the remaining drop/pickup handlers are unfinished.
- `Boons/BoonGeometry`, `BoonAbilityGeometry`: pure world-coordinate implementations of all original conditional patterns, weapon forms and passive ability modifiers, including composed forms/branches, strict corner rules, first contact, bump exceptions and concealed-body handling. Differential checks cover 5,312 original scenarios. These helpers now drive native owned attack shapes, damage and typed conditional previews through `TacticalAttackBoons`. Mirror routing and client presentation remain unfinished.
- `Boons/BoonEvent`, `BoonConditions`, `BoonChainHistory`, `BoonDispatch`, `BoonEventValidation`: immutable causal event snapshots, all 60 condition evaluations, original short-circuit side effects, owner/catalog dispatch order, pair/group/action/windup tracking, chance-roll ordering and activation ancestry. Transient views reference actual world actors; health/position are not duplicated. Visibility takes the observing owner explicitly. Dispatch state and activation snapshots round-trip through native JSON and reject malformed identities/history. Pruning retains chains referenced by pending work. The native world save contains dispatch/effect state. `TacticalBoonEvents` now connects native command events and scheduled work to the resolver, as detailed below; the complete original gameplay/event ecosystem remains unfinished.
- `TacticalBoonEffects`, `Boons/BoonWorldEffects`, `BoonEffectTargets`: native world-backed handlers for 22 of 24 declared effect kinds. Includes damage selection, owned timed buffs, healing, ward spending, marks, regular-enemy displacement/stun, burning application/refresh/cleansing/consumption/extension, immutable locked echoes and shared-gold purchase refunds. Burning and echoes run on the same world timeline offscreen and persist in actual world saves; status damage can shorten downed bleed-out, and death cancels the victim's burn. Refresh preserves an existing burn's committed tick/interval while replacing attribution and keeping stronger power. Direct executor tests isolate individual effects; additional public-command tests now verify automatic activation, finite downstream chains and scheduling. `EnrichDrop`/`GatherPickups` still require the native physical pickup/reward conversion, and XP/support attribution, remaining terrain/world listeners and client wiring remain pending. The unresolved food-overflow branch explicitly refuses to choose an adaptation; excess dedicated healing retains its original ward cap.
- `TacticalBoonEvents`, `Boons/BoonCause`: command/scheduled-work transactions drain queued events before effects. Entire damage batches commit health before damage/death/hit events; immutable snapshots retain pre-hit health, status, neighborhoods, pattern origin, definitions and causal lineage. Attack, movement, windup and scheduled burn/echo events now activate owned boons automatically. Lineage prevents recursive self-reactivation and survives delayed work/saves; completed chains are pruned, and world validation checks actor/history references and pending attack causes. Reactions use each owner's private sight without changing the selected view or shared exploration. Party owners run first, with persistent-ID ordering within party/other participants; changing the leader cannot reorder RNG draws. Walking has no Dash ability/path; only actual dashes carry those facts. Movement queues `Moved` before per-cell entry effects, and crossing a snare triggers its hazard even when the final dash cell lies beyond it. Ring of Reversal primes from dodged locked tiles and spends its owned bonus on one later hit. Autonomous sentry/discharge damage suppresses ordinary boon dispatch; traps retain ordinary hazard procs. Reactive downing preserves already-committed recovery. This still does not port pickups, the full terrain/squad/environment listeners or client routing. Attack modifiers and dice/vow are now connected separately below.
- `TacticalAttackBoons`: owned passive modifiers and all seven weapon forms now affect actual native attacks and read-only previews. Typed previews expose deterministic damage, optional Vow payment, dice power (without predicting a face), weapon form and visible conditional pattern cells with hit/kill/dash/wait/return conditions and delay. Preview body queries exclude unseen actors; execution uses actual bodies. Pilgrim's Vow is an explicit immediate-attack option, spends two HP while leaving at least one, and adds damage before criticals. Fateful Dice rolls once on each committed immediate attack, persists the member's face/action memory and passes the face into damage snapshots. Skull uses existing regular-enemy eligibility and normal defense/protection; miss still pays recovery/Vow but skips critical RNG, attack procs and Hookrunner pulling. Placement, parry and delayed discharge do not roll or accept Vow. Duplicate-class members keep separate forms, dice and payment eligibility. Public original-game comparisons cover all seven forms, all modifier kinds (including dash path strike/priming through the event pump), conditional preview shapes and all eight faces. Mirror routes, utility/focus interactions, complete enemy profiles and client controls/overlays still need integration.
- `TacticalReferences`: pending work, activation snapshots, chain history, constructs and recent tactical events retain referenced actors during existing wildlife-history reclamation. Once references clear, collected old remains can be reclaimed and removed from tactical participation without dangling IDs. This is cleanup integration, not verification of native population scheduling: the imported recovery flow still has a global-combat freeze and must be replaced/adapted to approved natural migration/reproduction.
- `WorldRooms` and generation metadata: retain actual BSP/sanctuary room boundaries and identify enclosed WFC floor components before ruin damage/connectivity repairs. Corridors and exterior footprint ground are not treated as one dungeon-wide room. Room identity remains stable across chunk eviction and excavation. Natural surface outside authored footprints uses the original roomless-map fallback. Future blended Roag complexes must supply their original authored room identities through this same world-space contract; those complexes are not yet generated.
- `WorldTacticalSimulation`: converts recovery timestamps to existing calendar ticks without idle advancement. Existing world motion does not move registered tactical actors for free. Selected-party vision and shared exploration update independently of party leader order; saved split-floor positions are retained. All-down saves preserve the rescue window; final death prevents continuing with another traveller.

These are foundations and a world-backed replacement engine, not a finished native combat port. The client remains on the previous adapter to preserve unported behavior during construction. Existing class, timing and progression values are preserved; initial delegated Guard/recovery/calendar values are documented in [MIGRATION_TUNING.md](MIGRATION_TUNING.md) and persisted in native state. XP weighting and remaining tuning are pending.
