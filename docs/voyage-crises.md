# Counted vessel work and physical voyage crises

These are optional opportunities, not new departure chores. The initial
equipped courier can still press E at the gangplank to enter Hearthford.

## Physical work

| Position | Interaction and cost | Persistent consequence |
|---|---|---|
| R, lower (20,15,-1) or main (50,5,0) | E, F; one timber lot and two actions | restore three hull integrity, capped at ten |
| U, lower (8,15,-1) | E, F; three actions | pump that deck's sparse water; items and other reactions remain |
| G, main (8,5,0) | E, F; grain or salt fish and four actions | two health and fatigue relief; injuries remain |
| W, lower (39,5,-1) | existing fitting previews | physical, removable workshop parts |

Inspection and cancellation cost no time. The starting hold has no timber;
repair stock must come from trade, a material reward or drifting salvage.
Work previews show the counted stock. Existing treatment, packing, rest,
chart and tavern interactions remain available.

## Voyage families

| Family | Options and physical problem |
|---|---|
| Cargo-rail raiders | thief and guard seek a load; physical P or established repel/distract/yield responses |
| Rudder grazer | territorial animal; fight, controlled light, or carry a fish claim to G/H for bait; earlier quick responses remain |
| Answering hull | rare mineral resonance; anchor, crew counsel, or chart/lead-line navigation |
| Two-rail boarding | upper longbow plus climbing protector and flanker; dodge marked lanes, break sight or withdraw |
| Loosened hatch | lower thief retrieves a real shipment, net bearer covers it; interrupt or recover the same item |
| Parting stay | upper (55,10,+1); forty-action collapse warning allows the normal chart approach and three-action work |
| Silt shoulder | spend four additional actions sounding or accept two hull damage |
| Drifting raft | one rope use and three actions secure one timber lot; refusal leaves it drifting |
| Spilled lamp | galley oil and finite serving cloth burn; a counted water source, F extinguish, or galley cover can stop it |
| Working seam | lower repair (20,15,-1); seat the seam, then pump remaining water |
| Loaded bilge | lower (8,15,-1); intervene in ingress or pump with shared material controls |
| Disputed seal | institutional trust, one paper lot, two credits, or a detained cargo lot |

Each family also has one later stateful form. It appears only when current
campaign evidence supports it, and the voyage panel labels the cause, changed
rule and counters before commitment.

| Family variant | Causal input | Changed production behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Shortage skiffs | a real market shortage plus carried cargo | third lot-caller; concession can expose a second lot |
| Displaced breeding pair | completed regional aftermath work | two physical animals; bait costs two fish lots |
| Returning resonance | earlier lure outcome or fitted signal shutter | signal navigation takes two actions and records Jomon |
| Obligation claim | a positive institutional obligation | fourth boarder plus a finite pre-boarding settlement |
| Marked shortage lot | at least five physical cargo lots | thief selects the carried good under greatest demand |
| Crosswind stay | storm/gale or a weather-exposure-three route | second warned support; one more exposed work action |
| Returning silt tongue | a previously traversed route edge | two more sounding actions or one more hull damage |
| Fire-marked carrier raft | a regional fire history | four-action recovery yields wet timber and charcoal |
| Grease-soaked galley store | carried wool or charcoal | second sparse fire; one more exposed work action |
| Worked seam reopens | low integrity or a recorded repair | a second, wetter and weaker sparse seam |
| Thaw surge | winter travel | a second flooded lower-deck cell |
| Counterclaim inspection | a positive institutional obligation | one more trust or credit; paper remains a physical answer |

Variant selection is deterministic and zero-time. The active variant survives
save/load; resolution stores its ID and exact consequence under that voyage
number, then clears the active marker. Unknown or family-mismatched active
variants are rejected as corrupt state. Refit benefits remain composable: nets
catch the first shortage loss, keel shoes recover one sounding measure, and
cover/backstay/strainers recover one exposed hazard-work action.

P begins a declared crisis, without an immediate enemy shot. Movement, hatches,
guard, attacks, gear and confirmed work then use the common action clock.
Menus remain zero-time; an unresolved voyage cannot be bypassed by departure.
Initial boarders start watching, at least seven distance units from the courier.
They use ordinary perception, sound, pathfinding, cover, goals and ammunition.
Exhausted or broken boarders return to their actual entry or surrender after
failing to find a route. Nearby eligible crew can assist; off-duty adult
exposure remains nonfatal. There is no hidden off-screen crew death rule.

R accepts withdrawal losses: one counted cargo lot and two integrity, retaining
fire/water already present. Finished events record a bounded outcome and route
arrival. Materials, opened doors, deck actors and dropped items save separately
from ashore geography/populations. A deck defeat leaves possessions on Jomon,
not in the last region. Existing regional quests are not reset by boarding.

Generation is seeded by route, recorded voyage number, weather, cargo and
integrity. Most legs remain uneventful. The first leg retains the previous
three-family distribution; later legs add the applicable families. Water
ingress uses six bounded checks, not an endless fluid source. Materials retain
the 512-cell location bound and 64 nearby updates per action.

## Verification limits

Forced terminal fixtures are labelled in the live assessment. They exercise
real curses input, not natural frequency or a campaign economy. Eight physical
families share several underlying combat/work patterns; eight definitions do
not demonstrate eight equally strong tactical encounters. In particular, a
fire can burn out while the courier is approaching. The resulting damage is
retained, but the intervention window still needs normal-campaign judgement.
