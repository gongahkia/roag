# Courier progression and production

These are main-Jomon expedition systems. They do not change the Dullest Dungeon game inside the tavern.

The active courier has a 60-node tree across blades, reach and impact, bows, gunworks, devices, spellcraft, smithing, alchemy, navigation, and diplomacy. `P` shows prerequisites and effects. Roles provide two starting roots; later points are awarded once per finite milestone, such as a first regional return, trade, local production, weapon-family combat, craft domain, or social act. A courier can cross-train outside their starting role, but cannot buy a node without its prerequisites or an unspent point. Switch couriers through the existing household system to build different specialists.

Teaching is a physical, partial inheritance route. A courier with the Teaching node may teach a nearby named adult aboard Jomon. At the moored common deck (`T`), a courier can spend paper and two actions to write one learned node into a physical journal. Another adult can study that journal there if they know its prerequisites. A recipient can inherit at most two nodes through teaching and journals combined; a writer can preserve at most three. Milestone points, entire builds, and all spells are not copied automatically.

The arsenal has 72 physical weapon kinds, including 36 new blade, reach, impact, bow, black-powder gun, and device-kit kinds. New arms enter the same pack, equipment, merchant, regional-cache, aim, ammunition, reload, and ordinary strike systems as existing weapons. Black-powder guns use counted fictional charges. Smoke, pitch, lime, brine, thunder, and resin payloads use counted bomb lots. Existing enemy equipment and Dullest Dungeon cards remain separate. `A` opens the usual spatial target view; the preview states range, ammunition, and intended effect before a valid action is committed.

`D` opens 24 authored personal spells. The spellcraft branch grants four spells per node; a cast consumes finite mana and uses the same target cursor and sparse world cells as physical tools. The spells create fire, water, smoke, ice, salt, support, displacement, healing, and other spatial effects. Mana does not regenerate passively during combat. It returns through a six-action rest at a moored berth, an attunement draft, or a three-action vigil at a marked cave entrance while no opponent is nearby. Each courier may use a given region's cave-mouth shrine once per day.

`W` opens the recipe and flask pages. Portable work can be done outside the tavern; regional works provide shore stations; late vessel refits add a forge, still, and gunworks on Jomon. Smelting ore consumes a physical charcoal lot unless a learned skill spares it. Weapon fabrication consumes physical billets, timber, wool, paper, clay, or other listed inputs. Completed objects need room in the pack or Jomon locker, and a failed placement does not spend ingredients. Regional gathering is limited by local source stock, which is renewed only by a supplied day-boundary economy. The physical works and their active lots are shown on the regional interface.

Field flasks accept carried ingredient measures in freeform combinations. `F` lets a courier inspect a flask and pour it at a selected map cell or drink a prepared draft. A preview lists known reactions before a pour. Ingredients and effects are preserved as sparse material cells, so fire, smoke, water, coatings, structural failure, and delayed thunder can continue affecting couriers, enemies, and physical equipment after the action. The still and Controlled distil node allow one reagent to be removed from a flask. Witnessed reactions and tasted drafts become personal formula knowledge; returning through Jomon's gangplank shares it with the household. A practiced healing, attunement, or breath formula gives a small extra benefit on later uses. The system does not require a fixed recipe menu to mix.

At a regional works, an effective Speech score of at least 7 or the Work order node permits a paid, witnessed NPC production order. Ingredients and one credit are spent when the order is accepted. At the next day boundary, the named worker places the completed item on the ground at that region's works; it is not teleported into the pack. The order and recent completion record are persisted in Jomon's save. Pre-integration version-9 Jomon saves migrate their existing campaign and receive default progression/production data; no standalone Dullest Dungeon legacy save is imported.
