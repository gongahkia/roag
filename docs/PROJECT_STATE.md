## Bounded wildlife population recovery — 2026-10-25

An observed wildlife population retains its founder location, current home/individual, generation, deaths and a bounded history. Migration updates that same population's home; leaving a region or vacating the original home cannot create another copy. A hunted individual stays dead with its original remains and one-time reward. Recovery can create a distinct individual no earlier than three days after death, spending four actual wild-food units, with low pressure, a viable species habitat, an unclaimed chunk and a free adjacent cell. Food, pressure, clearing, obstruction and history/capacity limits delay the next attempt.

At most four due recovery attempts run per 50 ticks, ordered by oldest deadline then population identity so repeatedly failing early records cannot starve later populations. The registry has at most 2,048 populations; new wildlife materialization/recovery respects 4,096 retained wildlife actors and the existing 10,000-actor save limit. Each population retains at most four previous individuals plus its current one. Recovery pauses when that history cannot be reclaimed safely: untaken remains, outstanding memory/trust/quest/combat/work references and recent reward presentation are preserved. Only taken, unreferenced old remains and their dead actor metadata can be removed; residual personal cargo decays into the explicit loss counter, while already collected drops stay in their inventories. Identifiers are never reused.

Optional saved population membership and complete records validate definitions, homes, generations/deaths, deadlines and unique ownership of current/history individuals. Older compatible saves may omit the records and register existing animals gradually at decision cadence without replacing them or inventing past births. Legacy oversized rosters are preserved and stop additional materialization while above capacity. Loading itself does not register or advance populations. No content hash, generation or manifest version changes. `./dev preview recovery --seed 42` runs an actual hunt and three full simulated days; the private `world-recovery` fixture uses an explicit near-deadline clock setup to check pending/new-generation reload and original remains. This step passed Core (900), tools (52), all world audits and private native checks (24 mouse /42 rendered); VERIFICATION.md records retained fresh evidence and visual review. Long-world balance, consequential memories, weather/seasons and final stress/scope acceptance remain pending.

## Wildlife migration — 2026-10-25

Seeded wildlife can seek another natural home after wild-food depletion, heavy pressure, or a cleared/depleted home resource. A single shared planner examines at most 128 candidate cells per five ticks, within 96 tiles of each animal's search origin. Logical ticks and stable actor order rotate the budget. Targets must match the species' floor, biome and resource/ground rules, have usable food/low pressure, and reserve a free habitat chunk. Searches never discover the destination or create another animal.

The same actor retains natal provenance, equipment, cargo and body state, then walks through actual connected terrain. The existing bounded route engine supplies at most 64 retained steps, with a 24-tile/256-node local search bound; live edits, objects and occupants remain authoritative. Active searches/trips continue offscreen and on another observer floor. Blocked targets are retried/reconsidered; changed destinations are not installed as homes. Fleeing/defending, battle participation, rest and active foraging retain priority. Arrival does not also perform an extra near-only wander step. Normal local wandering after a completed migration retains its previous observer radius.

Optional migration state validates the reason, original home, search center/cursor, reserved target, timing and current route. Only living homes/reservations count toward chunk occupancy, so historical dead individuals and their remains are preserved. A destination marker prevents observation from also seeding another animal there. Compatible saves may omit the new fields. No content hash, generation or manifest version changes. `./dev preview migration --seed 42` exercises a real seeded migration; private `world-migration` verifies travelling reload, offscreen arrival and observing the same identity again. This step passed Core (873), tools (52), all world audits and private native checks (26 mouse / 42 rendered); VERIFICATION.md records retained fresh evidence and visual review. The population recovery layer above now composes with migration; long balance/stress acceptance remains separate.

## Wild-food stocks and habitat pressure — 2026-10-25

Party and unmanaged-actor emergency foraging now consume the same finite wild-food stock in each 64-tile chunk. Empty grounds reject new party orders without cost; already queued orders wait without generating output, and hungry NPCs cannot manufacture food. Actual authored resource nodes retain their separate stocks. Node gathering raises local pressure after completion, hunting raises it once at death, and actual construction/felling/dismantling record distinct vegetation loss or restoration. Cleared land reduces wild-food capacity; restoring vegetation restores capacity without instantly creating food.

Logical hourly recovery depends on biome, remaining capacity and pressure. Low pressure permits full recovery, moderate pressure halves it, and heavy pressure temporarily stops it; pressure decays with elapsed world time. Closed-form integration crosses these hourly bands without iterating missed hours. Inspection performs no saved mutation, terrain generation or actor discovery. Food, pressure, last settled epoch and up to 4,096 weighted land changes per chunk are validated saved state, bounded by the world's 16,384 chunk keys. Actions → Gather food explains local stocks and depleted recovery; opt-in diagnostics expose the same current view.

`./dev preview habitat --seed 42` exercises real forage, exhausted rejection and actual elapsed recovery in an explicitly low-stock fixture; `world-habitat` provides its private native counterpart. This layer passed Core (848), tools (52), all world audits and private native checks (28 mouse / 42 rendered); VERIFICATION.md records retained fresh evidence and visual review. No authored content, generation or manifest version changes. Compatible current-content saves may omit habitat data and begin a default budget on first use; earlier vegetation changes are not reconstructed. Existing inventories, identities, resource nodes and terrain are preserved. Migration now composes with these stocks as described above. Bounded population recovery now composes with these stocks as described above. Memories, climate and long stress/final acceptance remain pending.

## Physical resident occupations — 2026-10-25

Activated farmers gather authored grain nodes, woodcutters fell natural trees, and quarriers excavate natural rock within 64 tiles of home. They approach a real source, spend work time, deplete/change it once, carry the yield in their actual cargo and walk to home storage before depositing. Needs, danger and explicit player assignments interrupt the same retained job. Full storage accepts only what fits; the worker keeps the remainder. Changed/inaccessible sources release empty jobs or send unused carried ingredients home. These jobs do not consume the 64 player work slots.

Healers collect actual recipe ingredients from town stocks and carry them to a camp to make medicine. If no nearby camp exists, they first collect and spend the authored camp construction cost, build a persistent workplace and later repair it using carried town wood. Recruitment transfers only the job's owned cargo to the pack, with overflow left in a recoverable bundle at the actual location. Hover activity reads the current phase immediately; extraction progress and occasional quarrying/felling gestures remain live.

Occupation activation permanently removes that worker from hourly aggregate production, including while interrupted or offscreen. Unvisited workers retain their regional model. Separate named/regional production counters and explicit regional imports/exports support conservation across production, crafting, trade, delivery and recruitment. Emergency personal foraging uses the finite habitat stock described above; individual resource nodes retain their own authored stocks. Existing resident journeys carry activated workers near/far without creating another identity or teleporting cargo. Optional versioned job state validates costs, yields, progress, phases and joint cargo ownership with player orders.

`./dev preview occupation --seed 42` executes all four real chains; private native scene `world-occupations` checks harvesting, actual cargo, save/reload and home delivery. The new `resource.field-grain` uses existing artwork and the existing resource schema. **The authored content hash changes: campaigns from the preceding content revision are safely rejected; start an explicit fresh slot. No automatic migration or overwrite is performed.** Generation and world manifest versions stay unchanged. Long-run balance, ecology, consequential memories, climate and stress acceptance remain in progress; VERIFICATION.md records the executed milestone checks.

## Saves and boundaries

Default selected slot is `~/.vagabonds/campaign.json`; `--save PATH` selects another. An existing unreadable/invalid slot fails without silently replacing it. `--new` explicitly starts over; normal saving/quitting then replaces that selected slot. `--load` requires a save and cannot combine with `--new`. No real player slot is used by validation tooling.

World saves use format version three and pin the authored gameplay content hash alongside generation/sample versions. Earlier world saves are rejected safely; this development expansion does not migrate them. World saves pair the JSON manifest with `<slot>.chunks/`. Chunk snapshots are immutable SHA-256 files, flushed before atomically replacing the manifest. Save/load retains actors, party, shared body state/memories/trust, clock, reserved work/progress/assignments, structures/condition, inventories/equipment/cargo, incidents, arc/history, edits/discovery/storage, battle plans/order and random state. Generation v4 and the exact authored sample hash are required to prevent silent regeneration changes. Unreferenced snapshots are retained so backups of earlier manifests remain recoverable; automatic snapshot garbage collection is deferred. Back up both manifest and snapshot directory.

`--import-legacy PATH [--save DESTINATION]` validates/copies an old RPG slot atomically, exits and leaves the source untouched; it rejects self-overwrite, colony saves and field sessions. The inherited `lantern-rpg` format name, object IDs and asset filenames retain their identities intentionally.
