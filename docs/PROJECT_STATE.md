# Vagabonds project state

**Last reconciled: 2026-10-22.** Master reference for the independent fantasy RPG. Update alongside behavioral changes; [DEVELOPMENT.md](DEVELOPMENT.md) gives repository guidance and [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md) records executed checks.

Vagabonds is the fantasy party RPG extracted from Ithica at `c87d480`, now extended with a finite procedural world and selected systems from the replayed Ithica history. The approved integration direction is recorded in [INTEGRATION.md](INTEGRATION.md). This repository owns its application, Core, graphics, tests, assets, catalog and mappings; it loads no gameplay or assets from a sibling checkout. [ORIGIN.md](ORIGIN.md) records provenance. Archived Ithica guides under `history/ithica/` describe inherited source, not current gameplay.

New and loaded worlds begin paused. Play resumes time; clicking reachable ground starts travel immediately. Actions offers explicit one-minute/hour waiting; danger interrupts it. New ordinary runs choose a fresh seed unless --seed is supplied; previews/diagnostics retain deterministic seed 42. Conversations, reference/game menus, combat and focus loss freeze the clock; Party, Work and Camp panels keep time running unless explicitly paused; there is no offline progression. Fine movement operates within 100 tiles, while production and trade run regionally farther away. Recruitment creates a job vacancy. Complete road blockades change regional routing; shortages generate delivery requests and affect prices, and repairing roads allows supply to recover. Detailed and regional simulation share the same actor identities and cargo.

Town exteriors are grassy, with WFC structural regions around roads/plazas. Authored generation profiles select algorithms per purpose/floor and biome terrain thresholds. Settlement/site positions vary across the world; a minimum-distance road tree plus a geographic ring supplies alternate routes. Seeded gradient fields form coherent vegetation, rock and water. Footprints are generated before chunk slicing; visitation cannot change layouts. Edits/discovery survive eviction and saves. The inspector distinguishes layout families, raw generation and repairs. `./dev audit-world --seed 40 --samples 8` produces a JSON topology report and SVG comparison. The executed 136-footprint audit found six families, 136 distinct raw layouts and connected stairs throughout; this establishes structural variety, not encounter or reward quality. Stairs remain protected and tunnelling is horizontal.

A main arc accepts evidence after a patron dies, and can resolve through the original town or a different compact. Severe sustained shortages can explicitly fail it. The side arc never gates the final encounter. The world continues after its resolution or failure; defeating the selected final boss completes the run. A wiped party leaves a recoverable storage cache; deaths must be saved before either continuation choice is accepted. Generated successor heroes receive new stable IDs while earlier deaths and the changed world remain.

## Controls and tools

Characters and abilities use typed JSON under `content/`. Conversations display large facing party/NPC sprites, personality-aware authored dialogue, click-to-reveal/advance text, active-speaker bobbing and original synthesized speech blips. Volume/mute and reduced-motion controls persist in presentation.json beside the save slot (preview runs never write preferences). Recruitment/trade transitions follow their spoken replies; effects apply once per selected choice. Healers offer paid treatment and inhabitants can share stories/meals. See [CONTENT_AUTHORING.md](CONTENT_AUTHORING.md) for stable IDs, registered actions/effects, reference-safe editing and reproducible previews. Equipment, bosses, recipes, shared simulation and incident definitions drive gameplay. Journal/Tactics describes the final threat and current boss tactics. Party offers clickable portrait/health cards, authored item sprites, carried/worn indicators, supply icons and eligible revival/parting/abandonment actions. Work uses task icons, progress bars and assigned/available worker portraits. Journal shows boss/patron sprites and illustrated, paged history; ambiguous legacy text subjects use a neutral icon.

The cursor brackets its current map cell. Hovering previews an observed route in teal; clicking keeps the selected route in gold and starts travel even while paused. Clicking people, caches, storage or stairs finds an observed approach, then interacts on arrival. Moving actors use their visible interpolated sprite bounds for clicks. Panels and disabled controls consume clicks before map input. Menus and combat hide exploration overlays; drag-selected cells remain highlighted. Travellers and inhabitants slide between committed cells, face left/right with horizontal movement and retain facing on vertical movement. Stairs, loads and long transfers reset interpolation; collision, costs and the simulation clock remain grid-based.

The **4096×4096 world is retained**. Exploration uses a close default camera of 36–64 pixels per tile, with a 24-pixel minimum and at most 48 columns / 32 rows even on larger displays. +/- buttons or the mouse wheel zoom; View restores the default. The separate World map shows known places. Outdoor terrain and inhabitants are visible throughout the local camera view, including behind walls. Only the observed area joins discovery; distant places stay unknown. Underground retains radius/line-of-sight fog and remembered dim terrain. Routes require observed, walkable cells. Partial edge tiles/sprites are clipped to the map.

World gameplay is mouse-only; keyboard movement and hotkeys are disabled. Play/Pause, Save, Load and Menu remain visible. Bottom buttons offer Party, Journal, World map, Actions, Work and Stop. Actions contains waiting and gathering. Left-drag selects up to 256 observed tiles; right-click opens actions at the cursor. Cursor menus display missing supplies directly. Excavation uses work time, fatigue and ordinary hunger; it reserves no food per tile. Build, dig, harvest and repair act on the selection; camps offer rest/crafting, people offer relevant conversations/evidence/delivery, and queued work offers suspension/resumption/cancellation. Construction, worker assignment/cancellation, recipes, storage transfers and trade have clickable choices. Right-click closes panels or stops travel and opens a map context menu. Dismissal consumes its click; context menus and dragging freeze the clock. Party, Work and Camp keep time running, expose Play/Pause and yield to new combat automatically. In combat, click an ability then its target; self/group abilities execute immediately. Menu contains the guide, inspector, fullscreen and Save and quit. Endings offer clickable continuation/new-world choices. [RPG.md](RPG.md) has the complete guide.

```sh
./vagabonds.sh --new --seed 1337
./vagabonds.sh --scene world
./vagabonds.sh --scene world-site
./vagabonds.sh --scene world-boss
./vagabonds.sh --scene world-battle
./vagabonds.sh --scene world-inspector
./vagabonds.sh --world-headless
./vagabonds.sh --world-report
./vagabonds.sh --validate-worlds 1000 --seed -500
./vagabonds.sh --world-smoke --frames 360 --screenshot artifacts/world.png
./vagabonds.sh --scene atlas
```

Default window 1440×900, minimum 1024×720. Explicit scenes, `--ui-report PATH` diagnostics and frame-limited captures ignore saves and disable automatic saving; explicit `--load` reads the selected slot. The Save button saves explicitly; successful Load resumes autosaving. World Load accepts only compatible world saves; restart to open a legacy slot. Invalid loads preserve the current session, route, panel and autosave mode. Headless/report/seed checks do not read or write player saves.

World party formation is authoritative: every non-dead member, including a downed companion, occupies a different walkable tile. Followers take at most one cardinal step per leader step, using observed terrain; followers round corners without swapping through each other. Right-click the last companion and choose Lead to reverse in a one-tile corridor without moving anyone. Recruitment retains a companion's nearby position where possible. Stairs and retreat choose connected landing cells for everyone before moving anyone, and blocked transfers leave the party untouched. Enemies can contact any member. Removing the leader promotes the next member at their current tile. Each sprite has its own movement interpolation and facing.

Local collision uses a shared Core object query for both rendering and movement. Non-dead characters, solid landmark furniture, unopened caches, barrels, storage and camps block entry; roads, doors, stairs, bridges and flat decorations remain walkable. Party steps, follower paths, local NPCs/workers, nearby caravans and stair landings respect occupancy. Hover routes refresh when visible occupancy changes and never consult hidden actors. Generated/older-save local actors on newly solid cells are placed on nearby free tiles deterministically in memory. Dismantled barrels use persistent chunk edits and cannot respawn. Distant regional movement retains coarse simulation outside the local area.

Compatible world saves from before this formation change may contain the old whole-party stack at the leader's cell. Loading spreads that exact representation across connected walkable cells deterministically, without consuming time/randomness or rewriting the slot. Already separated formations retain exact saved continuation. Malformed partial overlaps and enclosed landings without enough room fail safely. Formation and work additions preserve the gameplay content hash and world/generation versions. Optional rally/priority/assignment/suspension fields default safely in older compatible v3 saves. Presentation-only dialogue has a separate hash; mechanics still require an exact match.

## Legacy compatibility

Existing `lantern-rpg` v1/v2 slots remain playable through the original client with original maps, IDs, rules and exact continuation. Four fixed companions explore Hearth 36×26, Ashwood 48×32 and Sunken Abbey 51×37; iron key → seal → Abbot → relic → return. Defeat retains the old recovery rules. No world migration rewrites these saves. Explicit town/forest/crypt/battle/victory/atlas scenes and the old headless/report/seed/native checks retain this mode. [LEGACY_RPG.md](LEGACY_RPG.md) documents its controls, including Q quit and F2 atlas.

The generic Core `Campaign.Load` retains inherited field compatibility for source callers. Legacy startup, F9 and import use `Campaign.LoadRpg`, which rejects lunar field sessions. World startup dispatches by format and rejects them as well. Failed load attempts do not install a replacement session.

## Saves and boundaries

Default selected slot is `~/.vagabonds/campaign.json`; `--save PATH` selects another. An existing unreadable/invalid slot fails without silently replacing it. `--new` explicitly starts over; normal saving/quitting then replaces that selected slot. `--load` requires a save and cannot combine with `--new`. No real player slot is used by validation tooling.

World saves use format version three and pin the authored gameplay content hash alongside generation/sample versions. Earlier world saves are rejected safely; this development expansion does not migrate them. World saves pair the JSON manifest with `<slot>.chunks/`. Chunk snapshots are immutable SHA-256 files, flushed before atomically replacing the manifest. Save/load retains actors, party, shared body state/memories/trust, clock, reserved work/progress/assignments, structures/condition, inventories/equipment/cargo, incidents, arc/history, edits/discovery/storage, battle plans/order and random state. Generation v4 and the exact authored sample hash are required to prevent silent regeneration changes. Unreferenced snapshots are retained so backups of earlier manifests remain recoverable; automatic snapshot garbage collection is deferred. Back up both manifest and snapshot directory.

`--import-legacy PATH [--save DESTINATION]` validates/copies an old RPG slot atomically, exits and leaves the source untouched; it rejects self-overwrite, colony saves and field sessions. The inherited `lantern-rpg` format name, object IDs and asset filenames retain their identities intentionally.

## Projects, assets and scope

The solution contains Core (world/legacy rules, content, generation, persistence and simulation), Graphics (sprites/text/camera), Client (world/legacy presentation/input/atlas), Tools (graphics-free authoring/preview commands) and Core.Tests (graphics-free rules and client startup/reload tests). All references and content resolve within this checkout. No colony game is shipped.

All 7,115 occupied crops / 11,887 catalog records, 664 original PNGs / three archives and 22 derived reference images remain local. Original IDs, artwork, provenance, uncertain identities and measured colors are preserved. The canonical schema-5 catalog is tracked as `assets/catalog.json.gz`; the readable export is ignored. Historical `ithica-expedition-bindings.json`, `ithica-generation.json` and runtime manifest names are intentional. New structural samples are original authoring inputs in `assets/world/samples.json`. Read [ASSETS.md](ASSETS.md) before changing sprite mappings.

This is a playable integration, with limits to its simulation fidelity. Party members and background actors share body rules: food reduces hunger, exhaustion slows combat/work, large hits and incapacitation cause wounds, and rest/treatment recover them. Classes/levels/equipment retain their RPG effects; traits modify work/fatigue. Hunger reduces health to one rather than silently killing an unattended actor. Background regional gathering remains coarse. Explicit resource orders for party or assigned inhabitants deplete individual nodes; underground supply hauling remains outside this implementation. Residents keep one identity and their job/history/equipment on recruitment or parting ways at home. Assigned workers leave their ordinary production while occupied.

Construction/digging apply terrain only after actual work. Costs are reserved, cancelled/invalidated jobs return them once, and dismantling returns recorded construction costs. NPC workers approach through local paths; party workers independently approach targets along observed free ground and obey solid occupancy. Up to 64 work orders reserve supplies individually; unaffordable or invalid selected cells are skipped with reasons. Work is dispatched by Core every tick. Available companions claim distinct reachable jobs within a saved 16-tile rally radius, with priority, optional named assignments and suspension/resumption. Idle members return toward the rally without teleporting. Travel temporarily takes precedence and moves the rally with the party; inspection gestures retain orders. Stop suspends party work. Retreat suspends work so companions cannot immediately walk back into danger. Right-click orders to resume/suspend/cancel, or inspect them in Work. Workers refuse unsafe fatigue, hunger and injury. Camps/structures wear; repairs and crafting are optional support for expeditions. Incidents depend on actual hunger, exhaustion, wounds, work and trust, with saved independent randomness and cooldowns. These are bounded social rules rather than a full dialogue/relationship simulation. Regional food warnings include low reserves as well as immediate unmet demand.

Distant people still use regional production rather than individual hauling; autonomous food gathering is coarse, local routines are simple, and workers do not navigate stairs automatically. Arc families retain their evidence/return structure as optional side stories. Tactical walking/range/cover, directional animation, gamepad support and a sample painting editor remain outside this implementation. Chunk generation is synchronous and may cause hitches. Structural audits establish connectivity/diversity, and encounter previews establish executable behaviours; long-form balance and enjoyment still require playtesting. [WORLD.md](WORLD.md) describes the architecture and research sources.


The five integration checkpoints are implemented and locally verified; INTEGRATION.md records the scope. Integration acceptance included 210 .NET tests, 38 catalog tests, published-world/legacy headless checks and 15 native input checks. Subsequent mouse/camera acceptance is recorded in VERIFICATION.md. The current world uses manifest v3 and generation v4; the party formation fix preserves those versions and the content hash, so compatible current saves remain usable. The 136-footprint structural report and graphical captures are development artifacts under ignored `artifacts/integration/`. The local Ithica reference stays there for future development and is neither published nor needed by the game.

## Expedition expansion in progress

[EXPEDITION_SYSTEMS.md](EXPEDITION_SYSTEMS.md) tracks the full approved expansion. Stage one adds natural apple/cherry trees, fishing banks, wild nests, marsh herbs, honey trees, cave mushrooms and finite iron deposits. Resource types depend on biome, depth, terrain and a stable coordinate hash; only harvested stock occupies save data. Hover shows remaining gathers, yields and recovery. Depleted nodes have a different sprite; renewable nodes refill only after their last gather plus the displayed game-time delay. Ore never regrows. Clearing or building over a node removes it. Inspection never creates stock or consumes randomness.

Right-click or drag-select resources and choose Gather resources for one gather per node; ordinary automatic work assignment distributes the jobs. A pickaxe and fishing rod begin in the shared pack and can also be crafted at camps. Equip them in the separate tool slot for +60% digging/mining or +75% fishing work speed. Tools are optional. Party → Shared pack shows ingredient icons and counts. The generic forage command remains an emergency source of food. Cooperative combat and the final integrated expansion audit remain pending.

Resource definitions and the new tool slot change the pinned gameplay content hash. Old campaigns are rejected safely; use an explicit new run for this expansion. No migration or automatic replacement occurs.

Camps now support named cooking, treatment, watch and story duties from their right-click menu. Select a companion portrait, a role and a recipe/activity; supplies are reserved immediately and different duties run concurrently on separate work targets. Work provides suspension/reassignment/cancellation. Camp time runs normally and combat takes focus automatically. Ordinary camp rest lets assigned camp workers continue while the other companions rest. Damaged/dismantled camps invalidate pending duties and refund once.

Four ingredient recipes serve conscious companions within six tiles: apple/herb broth resists burn, cherry/egg skillet resists bleed, mushroom/meat stew grants armour, honey/herb tea reduces dread stress. Preparations expire on running world time, do not stack with their own effect, and survive saves; the Party/Camp portrait tooltip lists remaining durations. Treatment heals and reduces wounds. Watch increases initiative and starts subsequent battles guarding until its three-hour preparation expires. Stories react to recent memories and mark a real unknown site, with a per-speaker cooldown; their voiced, animated conversation and journal entry can be revisited. `--scene world-camp` is an isolated four-companion camp fixture.

Equipment now supports authored effects and seeded combinations. The Warden's shield blocks up to three damage for another companion at half health or lower, once per round; the Ember staff adds three direct power to burning attacks. Seven registered effects cover ally protection, burning, healing, dread resistance, digging, mining and fishing. Loot choices roll one or two permitted affixes on a base item, independently of map/combat RNG. The choice panel shows sprites, stat comparisons, named effects and their numbers; hover explains the full rules. Generated items keep stable saved identities through claiming, equipping, unequipping and storage, and cannot be rerolled or duplicated by reloading. They appear in Party only after acquisition, alongside authored equipment. The shield is also craftable with wood and mined ore.

Saves now include the generated-equipment registry and per-round shield usage. Validation checks allowed effect pools, original item statistics, ownership counts and valid inventory keys. Runtime `affix` previews execute every registered handler in combat or real work; recipe previews now supply the selected recipe's authored inputs in their isolated fixture.

Wildlife now has seeded habitats: rabbits flee travellers, spiders defend silk nests, bees guard honey trees and bats inhabit dungeon floors. Hover explains behaviour and pursuit distance; visible defenders warn on their gathering resource. Right-click offers explicit hunting; contact with territorial wildlife opens the existing turn-based encounter. Disturbing a nest/tree provokes its defender, which returns when the party leaves its bounded territory. Wildlife shares the existing needs/injury model. One potential animal per observed chunk is persisted, including empty chunks, so travel, save/load and eviction cannot respawn kills. This is sparse habitat-based behaviour, not reproduction or a full food web.

Defeated wildlife leaves walkable remains with cooking/crafting ingredients and awards six experience rather than generic gold/food. The reward panel shows nearby ingredient sprites and directs the player to collect them. Silk nests regrow on the resource schedule; silk and herbs make two medicine at camp. Wildlife populations themselves do not respawn in this implementation. Content/hash, actor habitat/provocation, observed ecology chunks and corpse ownership are validated on load; older campaign hashes remain safely rejected. Developer character previews now execute ecology, and `audit-wildlife` checks seeded habitats and real escape/contact outcomes.

Three new generated situations extend local discoveries: trapped storerooms, poisoned springs and guarded crossings. Their context menu and illustrated discovery panel offer authored approaches with explicit costs: disarm the latch, purify the source, pay the keepers or repair their bridge. Timed approaches use normal reserved work and automatic companion/NPC assignments, including cancellation/refunds. Challenging the defenders uses the existing turn-based battle; survivors remain provoked after retreat. Peaceful resolutions preserve the actual actor identities.

Every situation also names and selects a real side wall for excavation. Completing that normal digging job opens a walkable bypass and releases access to the cache, cancelling/refunding any superseded approach. Crossings repaired, negotiated or cleared in combat gain a persistent bridge tile. Outcomes, enemy state and the one-time cache claim survive saves; none controls global dungeon access. Trap/lever/fountain sprites and resolved colours make the changes visible. `preview discovery` now executes every supported approach for these templates; `audit-situations` checks all generated side walls/bridge sites and actual resolution/reward paths across seeds.
