# Courier specification and character sheet

New-world creation now opens a specification page before play. Choose one of the six seeded Jomon adults; the choice keeps their role, equipment, relationships, and the rest of the household intact. `J/K` selects a field, `H/L` changes it, `Enter` edits the name, `S` accepts, and `Q` returns to the landing. Each role offers a fully allocated starter preset, so `S` can begin immediately. Moving points from one score to another is optional. The chosen courier still begins at the gangplank at world time zero with their own issued kit.

Names must be unique among known people and contain 2–32 letters, spaces, hyphens, or apostrophes. Choose one of four original fantasy peoples: Human, Reedfolk, Stonefolk, or Tidekin. A specified Human courier's adaptability adds one more effective point to the competency named by their trait. Reedfolk gain one Fieldcraft and one sight radius throughout Greywash. Stonefolk gain one Craft and four carrying weight. Tidekin gain one Wayfinding and one sight radius in river fog or coastal squalls. These are explicit playable differences; none changes the identity of another generated person. Origin records one of the four established starting regions, and a chosen practical trait and origin each add one effective point to their named competency. Three named visitors represent the other peoples and retain their abilities if recruited.

The six attributes are Strength, Agility, Endurance, Perception, Intellect, and Presence. Scores begin at 6; eight points are allocated among them, with each score between 4 and 10 at creation. A modifier is `(score − 6) // 2`. Strength changes carrying capacity by two weight per modifier; Endurance changes starting health by two; Perception changes sight and Wayfinding; Agility supports Fieldcraft and can enable quiet movement at a high Fieldcraft threshold; Intellect supports Strategy and Craft; Presence supports Speech and mediation. The five existing competencies remain earned, capped at 20, and receive five starting points at creation. The displayed character sheet distinguishes base from effective scores. Dullest Dungeon continues to use its existing base Strategy supply rule; no Dullest code is changed.

Press `C` during world play to inspect the active courier's scrollable character sheet without advancing time. Talking to another visible person offers their sheet as well. Saves preserve the exact name and scores. Older saves load with neutral attribute defaults and keep their existing competencies; they are not retroactively forced through creation.

The design takes inspiration from [Caves of Qud's expressive preset-or-custom character creation](https://store.steampowered.com/app/333640/Caves_of_Qud/) and [Dwarf Fortress's adventure-mode backgrounds and personality customization](https://www.bay12games.com/dwarves/), while retaining Jomon's generated household and its original world lore.
