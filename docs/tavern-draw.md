# Tavern Draw

The `P` chair on the south side of the new left-hand card table opens Tavern Draw. It is a separate game from the Dullest Dungeon box at the `D` table. Its 13×5 surface blocks movement, and three invited named adults occupy chairs around it. Off-duty adults may also gather there between hands. The courier and three NPCs are the four players; the NPCs bet against one another as well as against the courier.

The game uses a standard 52-card deck and five-card draw: each seat receives five private cards, everyone completes a first betting round, active seats may exchange any number of cards, a second betting round follows, and the best five-card high hand wins at showdown. The dealer rotates each hand. A player may fold, check when nothing is owed, call a bet, or bet and raise. Folded cards stay hidden. Equal best hands split the pot; an indivisible credit goes to the first tied winner clockwise after the dealer. The sequence and hand categories follow [PokerStars' five-card draw rules](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/games/draw/).

Jomon's small tavern stakes are a disclosed house rule, not a claim about casino play. A wagered hand has a one-credit ante, one-credit opening bets, and at most one one-credit raise in each betting round. There is no all-in, no side pot, and no rake; each seat must be able to cover its five-credit maximum loss before dealing. Bets transfer real Jomon trade credit from the courier and persistent, finite NPC bankrolls. NPCs begin with twelve personal credits each and are not automatically refilled. Every pot is paid out in full. No real-money purchase or automatic repeat exists. A free practice hand follows the deal–draw–showdown card rules without betting or payout, so a new courier with zero credit can still play. The explicit cap and voluntary next-hand step reflect the low-risk product-design considerations in the [Responsible Gambling Council's standards](https://responsiblegambling.org/for-industry/rg-check-accreditation/igaming-standards-criteria/).

At the lobby, `J/K` chooses an adult currently in the tavern, `Space` invites or removes them, `W` toggles free and wagered play, and `Enter` deals after three invitations. During a hand, `C` or `Enter` checks or calls, `R` opens a bet or raises, and `F` folds. In the exchange, `1`–`5` marks cards to replace and `Enter` draws; no marks means stand pat. `Q` pauses a live hand and returns to the tavern. Return to the `P` chair to resume. At the settled result, `Enter` clears the hand; a new hand requires a separate decision.

The NPC policy sees only its own five cards, the public wagers, and its own random state. It keeps made hands, exchanges weak cards, and uses a bounded mixture of value bets, calls, folds, and occasional bluffs. It does not read the courier's hidden hand. The deck order, decision state, NPC balances, and a short hand ledger are stored in Jomon's save. Starting a hand advances one Jomon action; card and betting decisions do not advance the world clock. The three NPCs remain seated while a live hand is paused. An unfinished hand belonging to a previous courier is forfeited if a new courier opens the table.
