# Regional questlines and the working-marks arc

This milestone authors quest state directly in `jomon.quests`; it does not add
a quest language. Each regional line reuses its material objective as an
opening stage, offers a named optional cache, then asks for a regional decision
whose consequences persist in terrain, markets, contacts, or patrol ecology.

| Region | Line | Optional lead | Final decisions | Persistent result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hearthford | The Mill Race Compact | Flood-islet reed cache | public sluice compact / private reeve charter | later mill access and lower demand / credit and a divided mill relationship |
| Greywash | The Ledger Beneath the Ebb | low-road wreck locker | safer delayed salt road / disputed ebb salvage | longer tide windows / more stock and credit with registrar friction |
| Greenwold | A Fire Kept to Its Bounds | canopy watch cache | preserve medicine coppice / expand charcoal burn | smoke patrols withdraw / more fuel stock and a smokier timber economy |
| Whitecairn | The Honest Bell | ridge bridge coffer | close the unstable face / expose the false toll | alarm lookout withdraws / disputed quarry traffic reopens |

Accepting, refusing, or materially altering the opening objective all continue
to the regional decision. Refusal therefore has a consequence without silently
making the line unwinnable. The risky Greywash and Whitecairn endings require
either the named optional cache or a relevant environmental intervention.

Every primary contact marks one cache when the opening decision is made. A
secondary named worker can mark another, and gaining elevation can reveal a
third. These are persistent, credible map-memory marks; they do not reveal
moving actors. Secondary workers also teach one practical regional technique
or treat one persistent injury for field-care support or a recorded credit.

## The Four Working Marks

Completing any two regional lines unlocks a grounded cross-region account.
The courier begins it with a trusted primary contact, then physically carries
the record through Greywash, Greenwold, Whitecairn, and Hearthford. Decisions
at each chapter select disputed or cooperative evidence. The final settlement
has three trade-offs:

- an open carriers' compact lowers known route cargo risk and material demand;
- Jomon household surety grants credit but retains regional route risk;
- local control improves contact authority while preserving distinct markets.

Quest stages, decisions, evidence, cache marks, contact memories, and regional
changes are part of save format 6. Loading does not relock an available arc.
