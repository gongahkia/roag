Shared proposal: item controls, costs and room frequency
  • Controls: adjacent visible objects get a labelled Interact prompt. Use a choice menu with Confirm/Cancel. Inspection and cancellation are free; a successful service or utility use spends one normal speed-scaled action (nominally 100 world time). This is a proposed interface, not an existing hotkey.
  • One utility slot: flask, scroll or token, shown in an Items section of the existing I screen. No equipment redesign and no silent replacement. Planting a return token commits its one charge; leaving that room forfeits the planted return. Comment on any carried-item card if you prefer immediate-use supplies.
  • Frequency: propose at most two new service/supply nodes plus one new puzzle/hazard assembly on a floor. Signs are informational. Not every concept appears on every floor; do not duplicate these rewards at every existing prop or cut normal chests without pacing evidence.
  • Timing: strict enemy scheduling, locked attack cells, hazards, friendly fire and ordinary recovery remain. A menu or a scroll does not guarantee a safe reaction action.
  • Saves: offers, prices, used state, charges, puzzle progress and pending impacts belong in deterministic run/floor data. Reopen, stairs and Continue must not reroll or refill them. Optional interactions never block the mandatory route.
  • Numbers are starting proposals. Before calling implementation done: deterministic seed/offer and generation checks, real legal-action interactions, cancel/invalid-use checks, boon removal/stack tests and separate-process Save/Continue. LÖVE-window tests remain off at your request.
