# Aftermath contract presentation

A writing pack may edit `aftermath_text.json` titles, cause templates, and opening text. It must preserve all contract IDs and declared placeholders. It may not edit physical-copy rules, action requirements, rewards, costs, state changes, or contract progression.

For example, a supply cause may rewrite `{dependency} stock is {stock} after {aftermath_title}; ... {site}` while retaining all four placeholders.
