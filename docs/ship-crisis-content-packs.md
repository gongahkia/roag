# Ship-crisis content packs

`ship_crisis_text.json` supplies the player-facing words for the stable deck-crisis mechanics in `vessel.json` and `ship_crises.py`. A writing-focused author may revise crisis titles and descriptions, response labels, deck-work guidance, generated crisis-actor display names, intent text, alarms, work outcomes, and completion records.

Do not change semantic keys or template placeholders. They are the engine contract. The pack cannot change crisis families, triggers, response keys, stations, actor roles, costs, cargo, damage, material state, availability, ordering, RNG, voyage outcomes, or saved mechanical state. Use existing vessel, travel, character, item, and UI presentation sources for terms those systems already own.
