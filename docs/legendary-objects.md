# Historical working objects

Jomon generates one finite named object for each major region. This is not an
adjective-chain loot system: a short name is selected deterministically, while
the object records a real maker/contact, working institution, crisis event,
repair account, disputed claim and the existing cache in which it rests.

The current eight bases are a staff, weighted net, hand axe, heavy crossbow,
pike, war hammer, staff sling and spear. Their flood, fire or support-loss
history adds an actual handling property. Region-specific construction then
changes prepared range, difficult footing, material work, item wear, carried
weight and attack noise through the same production reducers used by ordinary
equipment. Every benefit therefore crosses at least inspection plus two of
terrain, combat, material interaction, encumbrance and environmental wear.

The clue appears as a clearly labelled rumor in the regional account. Opening
the named history cache creates exactly one physical item, in the pack if it
fits or visibly in the cache if it does not. Its complete provenance, major
effect, trade-off and interested institution appear in inventory inspection.
Opened old saves do not receive replacement objects, so lost or previously
passed-over unique equipment is never silently resurrected.

Derived manifests reconstruct from the seed and generation record. Physical
items persist their legendary identity in format 7; validation rejects missing
history, institutions, caches and dangling item references.
