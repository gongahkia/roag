# Regional-history presentation

A writing pack may edit `history_text.json`. Its named templates describe deterministic regional-history records, network-account services, and the history ledger. It may change wording only and must keep every key and declared placeholder unchanged.

For example, `"{witness} records a {crisis} at the {landmark}."` may be rewritten while retaining `witness`, `crisis`, and `landmark`. Packs must not change event IDs, RNG stages, region or institution IDs, resources, cache IDs, rewards, state changes, or service availability.
