# Finite procedural world

The world backend is graphics-free under `Vagabonds.Core.World`. `WorldPlan.Generate` creates the immutable macro plan; `ChunkGenerator.Generate` creates coordinate-derived terrain and coherently sliced footprints; `WorldSession.Submit` applies validated commands; `AdvanceTicks` advances the deterministic simulation. `IGameSession` and `LegacyCampaignSession` provide save/session dispatch without changing inherited campaign rules.

The surface has 64×64 chunks of 64×64 tiles (4096×4096), with three underground floors. There are 24 towns, six cultures/factions, six biome families and 48 adventure sites. Generation version two seeds and shuffles six biome provinces with smoothly warped borders. Connected orthogonal trade roads cross rivers with bridges. Natural terrain uses global coordinates. Whole bounded site footprints are generated before slicing, making chunk order irrelevant. Detailed simulation covers a 100-tile radius; terrain prefetch covers 3×3 chunks. The resident terrain cache is capped at 25 chunks, the footprint cache at eight. Persistent edits and discovery are retained separately.

`assets/world/samples.json` contains original structural examples, semantic symbols, culture palettes and explicit rotation/reflection permissions. The overlapping solver learns 3×3 patterns, their frequencies and compatible overlaps. Dynamic bitsets support hundreds of patterns. Town/site purpose and culture select a sample; four structural regions surround pinned roads/plazas. Every region has at most eight attempts, followed by a constructive layout if contradictory. Deterioration and component-connection passes happen afterward. Reports separate learned-pattern output hashes, fallback regions and repaired cells from the final layout. The old socket solver remains unchanged.

The simulation runs at 5 Hz; one real second represents one game minute. Pause, menus/conversations, focus loss and combat stop the clock, with no offline progression. New worlds and loaded worlds begin paused. People have stable IDs, generated names, backgrounds, traits, motivations, friends, jobs and homes. The player begins alone and can recruit up to three inhabitants; recruitment removes a worker from production. Nearby people follow work/home destinations and nearby threats pursue the player. Far towns produce and consume stocks; persistent caravans transfer actual cargo along regional routes. Full road blockades trigger rerouting or waiting. Stock shortages affect prices and history. Guard vacancies expose towns to faction raids. Promotion into the detailed radius does not create another actor or stockpile.

The existing four classes and abilities power encounters with one to four party members. World actor IDs are mapped separately from class/party slot and combat unit index. Enemies retain wounds after retreat. Survivors can revive downed companions with medicine. Deliberate abandonment and party wipes record permanent deaths; a bound current save is written immediately. Continuation is blocked while death persistence is pending. A wiped party leaves a recoverable storage cache. The next generated lone traveller inherits the same world, tombstones, history, constructions and economy. The main arc draws from trade, blight or succession premises, references actual sites/towns/patrons, and accepts evidence even if the patron dies. Resolution/failure leaves the world running.

Digging yields materials and consumes food according to hardness. Walls, floors, doors, bridges, camps and storage consume stack resources. Storage transfers preserve quantities. Edits invalidate navigation and regional route checks. Stairs link the generated floors; new vertical shafts are unavailable and stairs cannot be built over. Quests check visiting a site, so tunnelling is a valid approach rather than a specific door trigger.

`vagabonds-world` version-one saves pin the generation version and sample hash. The selected JSON slot is an atomic manifest referencing immutable, SHA-256 checked chunk snapshots in `<slot>.chunks/`. Snapshots are written and flushed before replacing the manifest, so an interrupted save leaves the previous manifest valid. Old snapshots are retained; automatic garbage collection is intentionally deferred so previous manifests/backups stay recoverable. Actors, clock, inventories, arc, history, combat, random state, edits, discovery and storage are saved. Incompatible/corrupt world data fails before returning a replacement session. Existing `lantern-rpg` saves retain their original reader and gameplay; field sessions remain rejected.

Research informed the layered design, rather than reproducing Qud's proprietary implementation: [Qud's GDC generation talk](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1025913/Math-for-Game-Developers-Tile), [original overlapping WFC](https://github.com/mxgmn/WaveFunctionCollapse), [independent chunk constraints](https://marian42.de/article/infinite-wfc/), [WFC design guidance](https://www.boristhebrave.com/2020/02/08/wave-function-collapse-tips-and-tricks/), and [fixed simulation steps](https://gafferongames.com/post/fix_your_timestep/). Solver code and structural samples here are newly written; no Qud art, maps or source code is included.
