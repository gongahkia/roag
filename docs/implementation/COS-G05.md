# COS-G05 — Procedural cultures, factions, contact, trade, and diplomacy

G05 introduces `factions=1`, enabled only for new campaigns that already enable industry, psychology, and personal knowledge. Older campaigns remain feature-off and have no foreign-faction state.

A G05 campaign contains four deterministic external factions. Each has an ASCII endonym/language identity, eight stable fictional culture values, derived dominant norms, bounded prehistory, directional attitude records, coarse stocks/capacities, and a current strategic goal. Foreign factions advance only through bounded campaign-level turns: they are not invisible fine-cell settlements.

A powered Signal Relay scans one unit per campaign tick. At 300 units it reveals one signal in fixed contact order. Contact is institutional but attended: a living, non-panicked representative begins a 60-tick audience, after which a three-step deterministic protocol is resolved with concrete actions. Personal familiarity is stored on the representative and travels with them. Culture remains hidden until contact/familiarity supports its presentation.

Trade is physical at the player boundary. A Trade Depot holds a 24-unit local buffer. Exported stacks must first be loaded into that buffer, then a powered Relay dispatches an accepted offer. The export leaves local ownership once; the promised bundle is an explicit 400-tick off-map courier shipment. Arrival requires the exact destination Depot and a powered Relay, otherwise it waits off-map until explicitly rebound to a replacement Depot at the same site. Foreign coarse stock is not local inventory.

G05 has no combat, raids, physical foreign caravans, generated real-world cultures, currency, remote teaching, or automated interplanetary freight. Hostile factions stop normal contact/trade, record their attitude, and do not spawn combat content.
