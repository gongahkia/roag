Approved environments — source-pixel verification

These images execute the real main.lua world draw pass, native image resolvers and derived material compositors using a CPU raster adapter. They are not LÖVE screenshots or a human playtest. Text is omitted, GPU antialiasing is not reproduced, and spectator visibility is enabled to inspect art. The original generated maps, entities, RNG and saves remain unchanged.

In the game choose Settings → Sprite Pack → Kenney mix or Kenney 1-Bit. Classic keeps its original shapes. F5 refreshes presentation. No new biome or boon is introduced. Reproduce without windows: python3 tools/environment_preview.py; verify byte identity: add --check.
