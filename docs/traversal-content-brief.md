# Jomon Voyager traversal content brief

Status: implementation reference. The Jomon Voyager is a fictional generation carrier travelling between isolated colony worlds on the route to New Edo.

## Fiction boundary

- Keep every traversal ability mechanically compatible with its existing tool, reward, and route contract.
- Describe equipment as field hardware, carrier issue, colony salvage, or locally improvised technology.
- Colony phenomena may be unusual, but do not imply a hidden conspiracy or a solved cosmic twist.

## Content families

| Family | Fiction | Implementation tone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breach tool | Portable cutter, auger, or charge for obstructed terrain. | Heat, dust, vibration, pressure warnings. |
| Line tool | Tether, grapple, anchor, or bridge spool. | Tension, secure points, and measured traversal. |
| Survey tool | Scanner, map module, or field recorder. | Signal returns, incomplete maps, and confirmed observations. |
| Mobility tool | Glide foil, impulse pack, winch, or deployable span. | Practical field engineering rather than superpowers. |
| Secrets and shortcuts | Sealed service spaces, abandoned equipment, or colony infrastructure. | Visible damage, radio noise, thermal traces, and route records. |
| Companion traversal | Landing-team coordination, hauling, scouting, and systems support. | Crew procedure and clear operational constraints. |
