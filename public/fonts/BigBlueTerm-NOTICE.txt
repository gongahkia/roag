BigBlue Terminal 437 Nerd Font Mono

This project bundles the unmodified `BigBlueTerm437NerdFontMono-Regular.ttf`
font as its medieval interface font.

Original BigBlue Terminal font: VileR
Source: https://int10h.org/blog/2015/12/bigblue-terminal-oldschool-fixed-width-font/
