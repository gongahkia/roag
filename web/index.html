        Colony wellbeing

        At 50% hunger/fatigue founders seek food/beds; at 100% health falls. Map dots: green eating, blue resting, red care. Farm plots need a planting order. Production waits at reserves of 20 raw food, 24 meals, 4 ore, 4 medicine and 2 tools. Start recipes from each completed facility. Meal: 1 food + 1 wood → 4 meals. Ore: 2 stone → 1 ore. Medicine: 2 food + 1 ore → 2 doses. A bed supports one resting person; an infirmary admits one patient. Duties: 0 off, 1 normal, 2 preferred, 3 first. Farming and hauling use gatherer duty.
