# FEEL-01 chamber / game-feel playtest

Play at least three Expeditions with at least two classes. Keep the `F3` debug
overlay off for the first two runs. This is a playtest of moment-to-moment
combat, not a score chase: record what actually happens—damage, kills, XP,
cash, modifier stacks, and environmental consequences.

Use quick timestamp notes at `0 min`, `2 min`, `5 min`, `10 min`, `15 min`,
and `finale`. Tag moments with:

`[SNAPPY] [SLOW] [CLUNKY] [TACTILE] [CRAMPED] [READABLE] [CONFUSING] [FUN] [POWERFUL] [RIDICULOUS] [GOOD CHAIN] [TOO MUCH UI] [LEVEL-UP TOO OFTEN] [LEVEL-UP TOO RARE] [CASH USEFUL] [CASH POINTLESS] [ONE MORE RUN]`

## Movement and camera

- Does one tile of movement feel responsive and settled?
- Does held movement feel fast but clearly discrete, with no delayed movement after release?
- Does the tight camera feel intimate rather than claustrophobic?
- Can you understand the small board, exits, walls, and hazards immediately?

## Chambers

- Does each room feel like a deliberate board rather than a small empty map?
- Is there ever more than about 20–30 seconds of uninteresting walking?
- Do walls create Force opportunities, lanes create pierce opportunities, water create electrical opportunities, and breakables/wood create explosive opportunities?
- Name one chamber that was perfect for the build and one that challenged it in an interesting way.

## Combat response

- Does a single attack feel satisfying before a build is online?
- Can you distinguish ordinary hits, Force collisions, electricity, explosions, component breaks, and kills?
- Does the close camera make impact feel better without hiding meaningful threats?
- Did any enemy attack feel unfairly offscreen? Note chamber/topology and class.

## Chains and real outcomes

- Did one action create a chain you wanted to watch?
- Could you understand its order: shot/hit/pierce/chain/kill/explosion/etc.?
- Did the compact receipt (`DAMAGE`, `KILLS`, `XP`, `CASH`, ammo) match what happened?
- Did a huge chain feel powerful without needing a fake score?

## XP, cash, and rewards

- Did the first level-up arrive within the first three chambers?
- Are level-up choices frequent enough, but not constantly interrupting combat?
- Did a reward offer a genuine reinforce-vs-discover decision?
- Is cash understandable, and did you make a real paid-cache decision (buy now vs save)?
- Did a multi-kill producing aggregated XP/cash feel satisfying?

## Explicit no-score check

Did you ever feel the game needed an arbitrary score, combo score, momentum,
style points, or multiplier? If no, say so plainly. If yes, describe the
specific missing feedback rather than proposing points by default.

## Debug pass

For a targeted final pass, press `F3`. Verify that the topology, chamber
number, view size, XP/level/cash, real action totals, chain count, board size,
and threat budget agree with what you are seeing. Turn it off again before
forming final fun judgments.
