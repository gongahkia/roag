# Post-DR-06 human playtest

This is the stopping point for the predetermined redesign work. Do not begin
another implementation tranche from this checklist automatically; the next
roadmap comes from human play feedback.

## Phase A — natural play

Start a fresh Campaign and play naturally for 60–90 minutes. Do not force a
route or try to prove every feature first. Record spontaneous reactions with:

`[BUG]` `[CONFUSING]` `[BORING]` `[FRUSTRATING]` `[WEAK]` `[POWERFUL]`
`[RIDICULOUS]` `[GOOD]` `[GREAT]`

In particular, note whether you wanted to explore one more zone, try another
build, abuse a discovered combination, establish a home, or recover a lost
body. Also record what repeatedly made you want to stop.

At session end, record only:

- Session duration; body deaths; zones visited; POIs entered; bosses
  encountered/killed; structures placed.
- 1–5 scores: movement feel, combat feel, build variety, body salvage,
  exploration, harvesting, construction, death/succession, world variety,
  desire to keep playing.

The most important questions are: **what made you want one more action, one
more zone, or one more build?** And: **what repeatedly made you want to
stop?**

## Phase B — targeted smoke

After natural play, deliberately check:

1. Four-way movement, held stepping, and an enemy bump.
2. Weapon/ability swaps, a magazine reload, and quick-slot feedback.
3. Corpse spatial salvage, supply walk-over pickup, and inventory drop.
4. Axe, Pickaxe, Cutter, and Drill; harvest a material then collect its drop.
5. Enter Build stance with `C`; cycle recipes with `R`/`X`; place rapid walls
   and a tactical barricade with faced `E` while an enemy is active.
6. Harvest → pickup → build; storage; reconstruction anchor; death and corpse
   recovery; save/reload.
7. Ruin, Reactor, discovery, boss retreat/return, and at least one DR-05
   synergy build.
8. Build during a boss encounter. Check that fire, Force, explosions, and
   electricity interact with player construction as expected.
9. Terrain remains visible without exploration fog.

Do not treat this as a replacement for natural play. It exists to identify
clear regressions after a normal session has revealed what actually matters.

## Questions for the major design review

- Is movement actually fun?
- Does combat feel responsive?
- Are overpowered builds discoverable and exciting?
- Does body salvage make you want to fight enemies?
- Is harvesting tactile or tedious?
- Is construction useful/fun?
- Does a base emerge naturally?
- Is exploration intrinsically interesting?
- Do zones feel like a world or disconnected boards?
- Are caves, ruins, and Reactors interesting enough?
- Is death/corpse retrieval compelling?
- Do persistent cleared areas feel satisfying or dead?
- Are bosses worthwhile destinations?
- Is the game leaning productively toward Qud, Minecraft, Zelda, or its own identity?
- What makes the player want one more expedition?
