How review, artwork pools and earlier decisions work
  1. Approve / Reject / Discuss the idea and each available look separately. Comments can change mechanics, sprite interpretation or assignment.
  2. Click sprite thumbnails to mark preferred cells (optional); unpicked artwork remains a pool. Changing preferences clears that look’s approval.
  3. Shared candidates are alternatives for several ideas, not reserved assignments. Selected actor collisions are flagged below.
  4. Missing 1-Bit counterparts are labelled; approving a coloured kit does not approve a nonexistent 1-Bit assignment. Shared 1-Bit art can also be used in the mixed look, in monochrome.
  5. Whole sheets, variants and multi-cell assemblies are grouped. A floor, wall, door, prop and icon are different roles. Art approval does not prove tiling or authorize changing collision.
  6. Earlier rejected spore pods/bombs and unselected moving carts stay excluded. Open On hold / excluded to inspect held art; it cannot be approved as a feature here.
  7. Your draft autosaves in this browser when supported. Download partial feedback at any time. Import validates its version, source fingerprint, decisions and sprite selections before replacing your draft.

Keyboard: use Tab/Enter for all controls. ←/→ changes ideas when focus is outside an input. Search and category/status filters narrow the review queue. Previous/Next navigates that queue.
