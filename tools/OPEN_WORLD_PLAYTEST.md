# Open-world campaign playtest

Use one fresh Campaign. Record observations in the brackets below; do not try
to solve issues while playing.

- [ ] Start in the Forest; cross at least two surface-zone boundaries.
- [ ] Descend to a cave and a deep cave; return to the surface.
- [ ] Find and use a service outpost. Leave and return: bought stock remains gone.
- [ ] Find a ruin entrance, enter its Dungeon interior, and return.
- [ ] Find Reactor access, enter the complex/core, and inspect its powered machinery.
- [ ] Find and claim a discovery site; confirm its access condition is understandable.
- [ ] Observe faction/ecology combat and a reinforcement source.
- [ ] Harvest materials, build a small shelter, storage, and a reconstruction station.
- [ ] Put valuables in storage, change reconstruction anchor, and verify the anchor is readable.
- [ ] Die away from the anchor; wake in a successor body and retrieve the old corpse/cargo.
- [ ] Enter a boss site, damage the boss, retreat, quit/reload, and return.
- [ ] Defeat a boss; inspect its persistent corpse/salvage and confirm the Campaign continues.
- [ ] Quit/reload after visiting multiple locations; revisit a service, discovery, structure, and boss zone.
