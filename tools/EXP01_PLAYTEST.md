# EXP-01 Expedition playtest

This is a prototype test of a combat-first disposable run. Start from
`EXPEDITION`, choose a character, and play one run without debug tools.

Record reactions in the moment:

`[BORED] [CONFUSED] [FUN] [POWERFUL] [RIDICULOUS] [TOO EASY] [TOO HARD] [TOO SLOW] [TOO LONG] [REPETITIVE] [ONE MORE RUN]`

## Natural play

- 0–2 minutes: Was combat fun immediately? Did first contact happen quickly?
- 2–5 minutes: Did the build change meaningfully after rewards?
- 5–10 minutes: Did a direction or synergy become obvious?
- 10–20 minutes: Did encounter pressure become denser/different rather than merely spongey?
- Final stretch: Did your build feel powerful enough for the escalating chaos?

Most important questions:

- Did any stretch have no meaningful decision? How long?
- Did a pickup make you immediately want to see what it did?
- Did a combo make you laugh or feel overpowered?
- Did later encounters feel harder/different?
- When the run ended, did you want another class/build immediately?

## Targeted smoke

After the natural run, check:

1. Gunner and Bruiser start unlocked; Conductor and Demolitionist explain their unlocks.
2. Each character has readable E/Q actions with the same controls.
3. Every encounter family appears: Swarm, Crossfire, Pincer, Hazard, Elite Hunt, Reinforcement Pressure.
4. The first reward arrives after encounter one.
5. A 1-of-3 choice pauses safely and shows current/next stack count.
6. A paid cache grants a random passive and spends only run-local SCRAP.
7. Duplicate passive stacks visibly change projectile count, pierce, Force, magazine sustain, or reactive effects.
8. Hazard arenas make water/electricity, walls/Force, or cover matter.
9. Elite clear feels materially better than an ordinary clear.
10. `I` shows Run Build rather than Tetris inventory.
11. Death ends the Expedition and shows the run summary; it never invokes Sandbox succession.
12. A Stage-2 unlock persists into a later Expedition without changing Sandbox saves.
13. Final boss retains body components, telegraphs, and localized break behavior.
14. PLAY-01 facing, threat footprints, tracers, hit-stop, damage numbers, and outlines remain readable under high-stack chaos.
15. Return to `OPEN WORLD SANDBOX`; confirm persistent Campaign save/world remains unchanged.

Do not treat this checklist as a request for another automatic implementation
tranche. The next decision is whether Expedition is genuinely more fun.
